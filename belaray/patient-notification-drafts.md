## 2. Patient email

**Subject:** An update on Healthfirst — and how you can help

Dear [First Name],

We want to keep you informed about your insurance coverage at Belaray Dermatology.

As you may know, a contract change on Healthfirst's side put our practice out of
Healthfirst's network in May, after 20 years of caring for Healthfirst members —
since the day we opened in 2006. We applied to rejoin immediately and asked
Healthfirst's leadership to reconsider.

This month, Healthfirst informed us that they have declined our application to rejoin
their network "at this time."

We were deeply disappointed by this decision — and we are not giving up. We are
continuing to press Healthfirst through every channel available to us.

**What this means for you:**

- Both of our offices remain open, and we can still see you. Text or call us at
  (516) 822-7546 and we'll go over your options, including self-pay pricing and any
  out-of-network benefits your plan may have.
- Belaray remains in network with many other insurance plans. If keeping your doctors
  matters to you, text us and we'll tell you which plans we accept.

**How you can help:**

Healthfirst answers to its members and to New York State. You have the right to tell
your elected representatives and state regulators when you can't get the specialty
care you need — and it genuinely makes a difference.

We built a simple tool that writes a personal letter for you in about 3 minutes:

👉 **keepmydoctors.com/belaray/advocate.html**

Thank you for 20 wonderful years of trust. We are fighting to make it many more.

Warmly,
Dr. Jeffrey Ellis and the entire Belaray Dermatology team
Hicksville & Stony Brook · (516) 822-7546

## Notes for the practice

- The messaging is factual and careful: Healthfirst made the decision; patients have a
  legal right to file network-adequacy complaints; the letters are the patient's own
  voice and fully editable before sending.
- Where patient complaints go, by plan type (the tool routes this automatically):
  - **Medicaid Managed Care / Essential Plan / Child Health Plus** → NYS Dept. of Health
    Managed Care Complaint Unit — managedcarecomplaint@health.ny.gov · 1-800-206-8125
  - **Commercial (employer / marketplace)** → NYS Dept. of Financial Services —
    dfs.ny.gov/complaint · (800) 342-3736
  - **Medicare plans** → 1-800-MEDICARE · medicare.gov/my/medicare-complaint
  - **Assembly letters** are routed by the patient's town:
    - Nassau towns → Assemblyman Jake Blumencranz (AD-15) — blumencranzj@nyassembly.gov
    - Suffolk towns → Assemblywoman Rebecca Kassay (AD-4, Stony Brook/Setauket/Port
      Jefferson area) — kassayr@nyassembly.gov
    - Patients outside both areas get a link to nyassembly.gov/mem/search/ to find
      their own member and can swap the recipient before sending.
- Consider printing a small QR-code card for the front desk pointing to
  keepmydoctors.com/belaray/advocate.html.
