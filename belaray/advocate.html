4 Have you tried finding another dermatologist?¿Ha intentado encontrar otro dermatólogo?

This part matters most — here's why. By refusing to bring Belaray back, Healthfirst is effectively saying its network already has enough dermatologists to see you on time. New York's standard is a specialist appointment within about 4–6 weeks. So try it: call the dermatologists in your Healthfirst directory. If you can't actually get an appointment within 6 weeks, Healthfirst's claim doesn't hold up — and that's exactly the evidence regulators act on. Only report what really happened. Esta parte es la más importante — y aquí está el porqué. Al negarse a aceptar a Belaray de vuelta, Healthfirst está diciendo en efecto que su red ya tiene suficientes dermatólogos para atenderlo a tiempo. El estándar de Nueva York es una cita con el especialista dentro de unas 4–6 semanas. Así que pruébelo: llame a los dermatólogos del directorio de Healthfirst. Si en realidad no puede conseguir una cita dentro de 6 semanas, la afirmación de Healthfirst no se sostiene — y esa es exactamente la evidencia sobre la que actúan los reguladores. Reporte solamente lo que realmente pasó.

Some services at Belaray are nearly impossible to find elsewhere in network: Mohs surgery with same-day oculoplastic (eyelid) reconstruction under one roof, phototherapy, and specialized hair-loss care. If you need one of these, check it above — a network that can't provide the specific service a member needs is exactly what regulators call a network adequacy gap. And text or call us at (516) 822-7546 — we'll help you get the care you need in the meantime.
